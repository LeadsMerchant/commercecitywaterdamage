Service Areas

Our free referral line focuses on Commerce City and nearby Adams County and northeast Denver communities. Provider availability can vary by day and exact location.

When you call, tell us your neighborhood, a cross street, or your ZIP code. We use that to try to connect you with an independent provider who works in your area.

Commerce City

Older south end, newer north side

The south end of the city around Derby, Rose Hill, and Adams City has many older homes, where aging supply lines, water heaters, and sewer laterals are common sources of trouble. The newer master-planned neighborhoods north of 96th Avenue, like Reunion, Turnberry, and Buffalo Run, have newer plumbing, but basements, sump pumps, sprinkler systems, and roofs still face the same storms and cold snaps. We take calls from both.

What “service area” means for a referral line

We are not a contractor with trucks. The independent provider you talk with sets their own travel area, schedule, and prices. If one provider cannot take your job, you can ask about other options when you call.

Outside these areas?

If you are elsewhere in the Denver metro, you are still welcome to call. We will tell you honestly if we cannot connect you with someone for your location.

Note: Listing a neighborhood here does not guarantee a provider is available the moment you call. Availability depends on the independent companies participating at that time.
