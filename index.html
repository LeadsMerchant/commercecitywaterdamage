What makes water damage common here

Colorado is dry most of the year, but the Front Range has its own set of water risks.

Hail Alley

The Rocky Mountain Insurance Information Association says the Front Range sits in the heart of “Hail Alley,” with damaging hail season running from mid-April to mid-September. Cracked shingles and torn flashing let the next rain in.

Sudden arctic fronts

On December 21, 2022, the National Weather Service recorded a 37°F drop in one hour at Denver International Airport, just northeast of Commerce City. Swings like that catch pipes in garages and outside walls.

Sprinkler and hose bib freezes

Irrigation lines, backflow preventers, and outdoor faucets with a hose still attached can split in the first hard freeze and leak inside the wall.

Basements

Many north metro homes have basements. A failed sump pump, a clogged window well, or an overwhelmed drain can put water on finished floors fast.

Creeks and the South Platte

Sand Creek, First Creek, and Second Creek all drain through Commerce City toward the South Platte River. Homes near them can check their flood zone on the Mile High Flood District or FEMA maps.

Leaks that linger

Dry air helps surfaces look dry quickly, but water trapped in carpet pad, framing, and drywall can still turn into odor and mold.

Sources: Rocky Mountain Insurance Information Association (hail season); NWS Boulder December 2022 climate summary (temperature drop); Mile High Flood District and city drainage studies (creeks).

Frequently asked questions

Is this a restoration company?

No. This website is a free referral service. When you call, we try to connect you with an independent, licensed provider who handles the kind of damage you have, whether that is water, mold, sewage, or fire and smoke.

Can I call at night or on a weekend?

Yes, you can call any time. Many restoration companies offer emergency response outside business hours, and we will try to connect you with one. We cannot promise a provider is available at every hour.

What parts of the north metro do you cover?

We focus on Commerce City (ZIP codes 80022 and 80640, plus the parts of 80601 and 80603 inside city limits) and nearby Brighton, Thornton, Henderson, Northglenn, and northeast Denver. Coverage depends on the providers available when you call.

What kinds of problems can I call about?

Common calls include burst or frozen pipes, hail or wind damage that let water in, flooded basements, sump pump failures, sewage backups, mold after a leak, and fire or smoke damage, including the water left behind after a fire is put out.

Is there a charge to call?

There is no charge to call this referral line. Any inspection or restoration fees are between you and the independent provider you choose to hire.

Should I call my insurance company too?

If the damage may be covered, it is usually wise to photograph everything and contact your insurer. A restoration provider can explain the documentation that is typically requested for your type of loss.
